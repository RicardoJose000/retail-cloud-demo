POS Connector Framework

Sync movements, not quantities.

Your test case: the POS goes offline, sells 8, an online order reserves 10 and a manager adjusts -2. Replacing one number with another loses data. The ledger keeps every movement and shows the real position.

  • Idempotency key per eventA resent sale is ignored, not counted twice.
  • Sequence check per registerMissing events are held until they arrive.
  • Oversell detectionShortfall hits the newest reservation, staff choose the fix.
  • Reconciliation, never overwriteMismatches become variance records with an audit trail.
